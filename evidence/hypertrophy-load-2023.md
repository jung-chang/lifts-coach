# Resistance training prescription network meta-analysis

- ID: hypertrophy-load-2023
- Year: 2023
- Topics: hypertrophy, load, volume, sets
- Confidence: Moderate.

## Findings

Many tested prescriptions increased muscle size; multiple-set programs ranked well. No single load range is necessary for growth.

## Applicability

Healthy adults.

## Limitations

Ranking does not prove a universal optimum; abstract reviewed.
